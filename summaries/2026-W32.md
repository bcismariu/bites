## The pattern that matters

Protein density, meal by meal, is the single clearest finding:

| Meal | kcal | Protein | Protein / 100 kcal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun breakfast (vinete, lipie, cașcaval, tobă…) | 982 | 43.9 | 4.5 g |
| Sat brunch (ochiuri, cartofi, pâine, dulceață…) | 1758 | 57.3 | 3.3 g |
| **Sun dinner (300 g chicken + spinach)** | **693** | **98.9** | **14.3 g** |

Meals are built around bread, vegetables and good fats, with protein as an
afterthought. That is why fat averages 112 g against a 65 g target while
protein lags. The fix is compositional, not about measuring more precisely:
**anchor each meal on the protein, then build around it.** Sunday's dinner
proves it works — it delivered more protein than the whole of Saturday's
brunch, in 40 % of the calories.

## Sleep & energy

| Night | Dinner | Training that day | Sleep | Energy next day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 07→08 | 22:15, 813 kcal, 40 g fat, very spicy | none | ~8 h, **2/5**, last 2–3 h broken | 2/5 |
| 08→09 | 20:00, 867 kcal, 28 g fat | **full gym + swim + sauna** | ~8 h 15, **4/5**, no interruptions | 3 → 3.5/5 |
| 09→10 | 17:20, 693 kcal, lean | walk + sauna only | ~6 h (22:30 → 04:30), **3/5**, woke and could not resume | 3/5 |

**The dinner hypothesis did not survive the third night.** Night 09→10 had
the earliest and leanest dinner of the three and produced the worst sleep of
the three. If dinner timing were the driver, that night should have been the
best.

**Revised hypothesis: physical exertion tracks the data better than dinner
does.** The one good night followed the only day with a real training load;
both poor nights followed days without one. Still three data points and
still confounded — the 09→10 waking has two candidate causes the user named
himself, a bathroom trip and feeling cold, either of which is sufficient on
its own.

Worth noting as a third candidate: dinner at 17:20 meant ~11 hours fasted by
04:30. Hunger is a plausible waking trigger, which would make *very* early
dinners their own problem.

Next tests, in order of value: a training day with a late dinner (separates
the two variables), and controlling room temperature and fluid timing before
bed so they stop being confounders.

Caffeine and nicotine are permanently absent (he drinks no coffee and does
not smoke), which removes the two usual confounders from this analysis.
