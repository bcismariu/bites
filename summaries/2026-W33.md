## Where the calories actually are

Not in sugar, not in snacking. In **fat**: 99 g/day against a 65 g target,
i.e. ~310 surplus kcal/day — almost exactly the missing deficit. Every
single day of the week was over the fat target, the best of them (Thu, 83 g)
still 28 % over.

The fat is not junk. It is olives, avocado, peanut butter, nuts, cooking
oil, fried potatoes, fatty fish, urdă, cheese — food that belongs in this
diet. But **good fat still costs 9 kcal/g**, and at this volume it is the
only reason the deficit is a third of what it should be.

Saturday shows the extreme cleanly: 135.7 g of fat, of which mici (40 g) and
fair chips (22.5 g) were 62 g between them.

no food or training signal in this week's sleep data at all.** The week is a
write-off for correlation purposes.

The one thing worth carrying forward is the **early-morning waking without
return**, which happened on all five nights the child was not the cause. Six
and a half hours is short, but waking at 06:00 and getting up is a normal
morning; waking at 03:30 and lying awake is not. If the pattern is still
present in another one to two weeks now that the household is back to
normal, it is worth a doctor's opinion rather than more self-experimentation.

## Three things for next week

1. **Cut ~35 g of fat per day.** That single change is worth ~310 kcal and
   converts a 160 kcal/day deficit into a ~470 one — the plan as designed,
   with no other adjustment. Concretely: skip the oil on already-fatty
   dishes, halve the olives/avocado/peanut-butter portions on days that
   already contain fatty fish or urdă, and choose 0 % over 2 % dairy by
   default. Do not cut the fish.
2. **Fiber to 35 g, using psyllium as the floor.** One teaspoon is 3.9 g for
   9 kcal — two a day plus one legume dish (linte or fasole, ~12 g) closes
   the whole 10.6 g gap for under 20 kcal. This is the cheapest target on the
   board and the only one still missed every day.
3. **Add real cardio to both gym days.** 20–25 minutes after lifting, easy
   pace, conversational. Aimed at visceral fat specifically, and it is the
   one lever that has not been pulled at all. Load-wise: shoulder press
   7.5 → 10 kg, lat pulldown all three sets @ 20 kg, seated row 20 → 22.5 kg,
   leg press 63 → 70 kg.
