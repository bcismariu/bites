# Food dictionary

Personal food database. Every food that appears in the log has a row here.
All nutrient values are **per 100 g** (ml for liquids); the portion column is
this user's usual serving. Source: `label` (see `foods/labels/`) or
`estimate` (generic USDA-typical values). **Key micros** lists the tags from
`foods/micronutrients.md` that a usual portion meaningfully provides.

`Sugar` is total sugar; `Added` is the free-sugar part only (added in
production or preparation, plus honey, syrups and fruit juice). Sugar
naturally present in whole fruit, vegetables and milk is not counted as
added.
